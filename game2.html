Grand Theft Auto V (GTA 5)

Release Date: September 17, 2013 (initial)
Genre: Action‑Adventure
Platforms: PS3, PS4, PS5, Xbox 360, Xbox One, Xbox Series X/S, PC

Grand Theft Auto V (GTA 5) is an open‑world action‑adventure game developed by Rockstar North and published by Rockstar Games. It features a sprawling world set in Los Santos and Blaine County, blending a compelling story with freedom to explore, drive, fight, and interact with the world.
