Call of Duty: Modern Warfare II

Release Date: October 28, 2022
Genre: First-Person Shooter (FPS)
Platforms: PC, PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Xbox One, Xbox Series X/S

Call of Duty: Modern Warfare II (2022) is a first-person shooter game that offers a globe-trotting single-player campaign, immersive multiplayer combat, and advanced gameplay innovations. Players engage in tactical battles, realistic combat scenarios, and strategic team-based missions.
